Log contacts in the field, straight to your own Wavelog.

A fast, big-button logger for Android, built for POTA and SOTA activations and park hunting. It talks to your Wavelog server directly, and keeps working when the signal drops.

Built for the field

Everything you need between “CQ POTA” and the next caller.

Type the call, check the badge, tap save. The rest happens on its own: lookups, parks, upload, and the logbook on your server.

⚡

Fast entry

A big callsign keypad, swipeable band and VFO dials, and reports that default to 59 / 599. Band, frequency and mode are remembered between contacts.

🌲

POTA & SOTA, offline

Search every park and summit by reference or name, with no signal. Add several parks per contact, or recall the last ones when two ops share a park.

📡

Straight to Wavelog

Contacts go to your own Wavelog server as they're logged. No signal? Offline mode queues them and syncs when you're back.

🔁

Worked before, at a glance

A NEW / WORKED badge for the call, band and mode. Tap “WORKED 5×” for your recent contacts with that station.

🔥

Daily streak

Your Wavelog QSO streak on the main screen, and a reminder at 21:45 UTC on days you haven't logged yet. Easy to switch off.

🗺️

See where they are

One tap puts the other station on a map, pinned from their callbook location or grid square. A built-in world map keeps it working when there's no coverage.

Getting started

Up and running in a few minutes.

You'll need a Wavelog server: your own, or one you have an account on.

  1. Connect Wavelog

    Enter your Wavelog URL and an API key. A v2 key (wl2_…) unlocks contact history, the logbook viewer and the streak. Pick your station profile.

  2. Download the park lists

    One tap, on Wi-Fi, saves the POTA and SOTA lists (about 15 MB) to your phone for offline search.

  3. Go operate

    Lookups use your Wavelog server's callbook, or your QRZ login. Then just log: contacts upload as you go, or queue until you're back in range.
